5. Holdings, owners and members

  • A holding is a farm business's space in the app, with its farms and fields. Whoever creates a holding becomes its owner. Every holding has exactly one owner.
  • The owner and the directors decide who joins the holding, in what role, and what each person can see. Anyone with a valid invite code can join in the role that code gives, so share codes with care.
  • The owner is responsible for the holding: its records, its members, and paying for it (see section 6).
  • The owner can delete the holding. That deletes every record in it, for everyone, and cannot be undone.

A holding's owner is the only person who pays for it. Directors, managers, workers, consultants and mechanics never pay for a holding they don't own. Someone who owns several holdings pays for all of them on one subscription.

  • If you own more holdings than your plan covers, the plan covers your oldest holdings first. The newest become unpaid (section 6.6) until you move to a larger plan.

  • Uninstalling the app or deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play.
  • If a payment fails, Google Play keeps trying for a while. The holding stays paid during that time.

6.6 When a holding stops being paid for

  • A holding becomes unpaid when its paid time ends without being renewed. That can happen because the owner cancels, because payments keep failing, because the owner's plan no longer covers it, or because ownership passes to someone whose plan doesn't cover it.
  • For up to 30 days, everyone on the holding keeps working as usual. The app shows a reminder every day saying when work will stop.
  • A holding gets at most 30 unpaid days in any 12 months. Transferring the holding does not give it more.
  • The unpaid period only follows paid time. When a trial or free time ends, the holding goes straight to read-only.

6.7 Read-only

A holding becomes read-only when its unpaid days run out, or when its trial or free time ends without a subscription.

  • Everyone on it can still view, export and back up its records.
  • The owner and directors can still subscribe, redeem a promo code, transfer or take over the holding, and remove members.
  • Nobody can add or change records.
  • A holding is never deleted because it is not paid for. Once payment is confirmed, full use comes back and nothing is lost.

6.8 Promo codes and free time

  • We may give out promo codes, or give a holding free time directly.
  • A code adds a set number of free days to a holding, after any free time it already has. The owner or a director can redeem it. A holding can use each code only once.
  • A code may have a limit on how many holdings can use it and a last date for redeeming it. Codes have no cash value and may not be sold.
  • Free time belongs to the holding. It stays with the holding if the owner changes.
  • A holding on free time doesn't use up a place on your plan. Google Play keeps charging for your current plan until you move to a smaller one.
  • When free time ends, the holding needs a subscription or it becomes read-only.
  • We may cancel a code that was obtained or used dishonestly.

6.9 Changing who owns a holding

A holding's owner changes in only two ways.

Transfer. The owner may transfer the holding to one of its directors.

  • The director has to accept, because accepting means taking on the payment.
  • The owner can cancel the request until it is accepted. A request that isn't accepted within 14 days lapses.
  • The old owner stays on as a director unless the new owner changes that.
  • If the new owner's plan doesn't cover the holding, it becomes unpaid under section 6.6.

Takeover. If a holding is unpaid or read-only, any of its directors may subscribe for it and become its owner in the same step.

  • Only directors can do this, and never while somebody is paying for the holding.
  • The previous owner and every director are told when it happens.
  • By owning a holding, you accept that a director may take it over in this way if you stop paying for it.
