Country Manager is an app for running a farm: its fields and crops, livestock, vehicles and equipment, stock and fuel, staff attendance, time sheets and pay, and the people who work on it. It keeps records and helps you plan. It does not replace professional advice (see section 10).

5. Holdings, owners and members

  • A holding is a farm business's space in the app, with its farms and fields. Whoever creates a holding becomes its owner. Every holding has exactly one owner.
  • The owner and the directors decide who joins the holding, in what role, and what each person can see. Anyone with a valid invite code can join in the role that code gives, so share codes with care.
  • When someone leaves the farm, the owner or a director must remove them from the holding (a director, by the owner). The owner and directors must also withdraw invite codes that are no longer needed. Both can be done while the holding is read-only.
  • The owner is responsible for the holding: its records, its members, and paying for it (see section 6).
  • The owner can delete the holding. That deletes every record in it, for everyone, and cannot be undone.
  • A member who leaves or is removed loses access. The records they made stay with the holding.
