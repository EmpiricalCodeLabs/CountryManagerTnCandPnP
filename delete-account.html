Delete your Country Manager account

Country Manager, by EmpiricalCodeLabs · Last updated 2 October 2026

You can delete your Country Manager account yourself in the app, or ask us to delete it by email if you can't get into the app. To remove only some of your data and keep the account, see Delete some of your data.

In the app

  1. Open Country Manager and tap More, at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Tap Account Settings.
  3. Under Close this account, tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm with your password, or sign in with Google again if that is how you sign in.

Without the app

  1. Email empiricalcodelabs@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to Country Manager.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my Country Manager account.
  3. If you can no longer use that email address, say so in your email. We will find another way to check that the account is yours before we delete anything.

We will confirm when your account has been deleted, within 30 days of your request.

Before you delete

  • If you own a holding, hand it over to somebody else or delete it first. A holding without an owner can't be managed by anybody, not even the people still working on it. If you ask us by email, we will ask you which you want and help you do it.
  • If you pay for a subscription, cancel it in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel it.
  • If you want a copy of your farm's records, a director or manager can take a backup from the app first.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted straight awayKept
  • your sign-in
  • your profile: name, email address, phone number, photo link and the record of the terms you accepted
  • your membership of every holding you belong to
  • the notification token of every phone you signed in on
  • the record of which phone and computer your account is in use on
  • the problems and ideas you sent from the app, with their screenshots
  • records you made on a holding, such as field work, stock counts or notice board posts, with your name on them. They belong to the farm and stay until the farm deletes them or its holding.
  • records of subscription purchases, for seven years, as Dutch tax law requires

Deleted information is removed from Google's backups as soon as Google reasonably can, and within 180 days at most, as Google's terms for Firebase require. The Privacy Policy explains how long we keep things.

Delete some of your data, and keep your account

You don't have to close your account to remove information from Country Manager. In the app:

To deleteDo thisWhat happens
Your phone number More → Account Settings → Phone. Clear the number and tap Save. It is deleted from your profile straight away.
Your place on a holding More → Account Settings. Under Your farms, tap Leave next to the holding. (An owner hands the holding over first.) Your membership, role and assignments on that holding are deleted. Records you made there stay with the farm.
Your phone's notification token Sign out on that phone: More → Account Settings → Sign out. The token for that phone is deleted. Turning notifications off in Android's settings stops them, but keeps the token until you sign out.
Which phone your account is in use on Sign out on that phone: More → Account Settings → Sign out. The phone's make and model, and the id the app gave it, are deleted.
A record on a farm Open the record and delete it. Whether you can depends on your role on that holding. It is deleted for everybody on the holding.
A whole farm (owner only) On the More tab, open the holding's menu (⋮), choose Delete, and confirm with the holding's name. Every record in it is deleted permanently, for everybody on it.

For anything else, email empiricalcodelabs@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, and say what you want deleted. We reply within 30 days. Records a farm keeps about you, for example as its employee, belong to that farm, so ask the farm first; we will help it if it needs us.

As with a closed account, deleted information is removed from Google's backups within 180 days at most, and records of subscription purchases are kept for seven years, as Dutch tax law requires.
