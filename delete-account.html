Delete your Country Manager account

Country Manager, by EmpiricalCodeLabs · Last updated 24 September 2026

You can delete your Country Manager account yourself in the app, or ask us to delete it by email if you can't get into the app.

In the app

  1. Open Country Manager and tap More, at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Tap Account Settings.
  3. Under Close this account, tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm with your password, or sign in with Google again if that is how you sign in.

Without the app

  1. Email empiricalcodelabs@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to Country Manager.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my Country Manager account.
  3. If you can no longer use that email address, say so in your email. We will find another way to check that the account is yours before we delete anything.

We will confirm when your account has been deleted, within 30 days of your request.

Before you delete

  • If you own a holding, hand it over to somebody else or delete it first. A holding without an owner can't be managed by anybody, not even the people still working on it. If you ask us by email, we will ask you which you want and help you do it.
  • If you pay for a subscription, cancel it in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel it.
  • If you want a copy of your farm's records, a director or manager can take a backup from the app first.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted straight awayKept
  • your sign-in
  • your profile: name, email address, phone number, photo link and the record of the terms you accepted
  • your membership of every holding you belong to
  • records you made on a holding, such as field work, stock counts or notice board posts, with your name on them. They belong to the farm and stay until the farm deletes them or its holding.
  • records of subscription purchases, for seven years, as Dutch tax law requires

Deleted information can stay in Google's backup systems for a limited time before it is overwritten. The Privacy Policy explains how long we keep things.

Deleting a whole farm

A holding's owner can delete the holding in the app. That permanently deletes every record in it, for everybody on it. If you need help, email empiricalcodelabs@gmail.com.
