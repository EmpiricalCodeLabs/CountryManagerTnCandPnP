Privacy Policy

Country Manager · Last updated 24 September 2026 · Version 2026-09-24

This policy explains what the Country Manager app collects, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and what you can ask us to do about it. Country Manager is used on farms in South Africa and run from the Netherlands. This policy is therefore written to meet both South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

In short

  • We collect what the app needs to work: your account details, and the farm records you and the people you work with type in.
  • We don't sell your information, show adverts, or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • Records are stored on Google's servers in the European Union.
  • Location is used only while the app is open on your screen. Where you go is never recorded.
  • You can delete your account in the app, or ask us to delete it.

2. Your account and your farm's records

Country Manager holds two kinds of information, and we have a different job for each.

  • Your account. These are the details you give us so you can sign in. We decide how they are used, so we are the controller for them.
  • Your farm's records. Everything recorded inside a holding belongs to that holding: fields, livestock, stock, fuel, the attendance register and so on. The business that owns the holding decides what is recorded and who may see it. That makes the holding's owner the controller (the responsible party, in POPIA's words) for those records. We store and process them for the holding as its processor (its operator, in POPIA's words), and use them for nothing else. The terms we work under are in section 9 of the Terms and Conditions.

3. What we collect

3.1 Your account

  • Your email address and password. Google's Firebase Authentication handles your password, and we never see it.
  • If you sign in with Google: the name, email address and profile photo link on your Google account.
  • Your name, the role you chose when you signed up (director, manager, worker, consultant or mechanic), and your phone number if you add one.
  • When your account was created and when you last used the app.
  • Which version of the Terms and Conditions and of this Privacy Policy you accepted, and when.

You need to give us an email address and a name to use Country Manager. Without them we can't create your account.

3.2 The holdings you belong to

For each holding: your role, the fields, vehicles and equipment you are assigned to, any extra records you have been given access to, who invited you and when you joined. Your name and email are copied onto your membership so that the people you work with can see who you are.

3.3 Farm records

These are whatever you and other members of a holding record in it. For example:

  • farms, fields and field boundaries; crops, planting, spraying and fertiliser records; soil tests, harvests and rainfall
  • livestock: animals, mobs, calvings, movements, treatments and sales, including the name of a buyer or auctioneer
  • vehicles, equipment, services, parts and job cards
  • the fuel ledger, stock on hand, prices and costs
  • suppliers and the names, phone numbers and email addresses of the people you deal with there
  • to-do lists and notice board posts

Every record keeps the name of the person who made it, so the farm knows who recorded what.

3.4 Staff and attendance records

If a holding uses the HR section, it holds details of the farm's employees. Most of them don't use the app. The details are name, employee number, phone number, occupation, the dates employment started and ended, the type of contract, the working week, and a daily attendance register with notes. The app does not ask for ID numbers.

The register records sick leave and whether a medical certificate was handed in. That is health information: special personal information under POPIA, and a special category of data under the GDPR. The farm keeps it to meet its duties under the Basic Conditions of Employment Act. Only the holding's directors and managers can see HR records.

3.5 Subscription purchases

If you buy a subscription, Google Play takes the payment. We receive a record of the purchase so we can tell which holdings are paid for. It shows the plan, the order number, a purchase token from Google, the dates, and whether it is active, cancelled or refunded. We never see your card or bank details.

3.6 Files you choose

When you import a spreadsheet, the app reads only the file you pick. When you export a spreadsheet or a backup, the file is made on your phone and goes wherever you send it. After that it is out of our hands.

3.7 What your phone sends

To work at all, the services the app uses receive some technical information: your IP address, the app version, and the tokens that keep you signed in. We don't use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. We don't build a profile of how you use the app.

3.8 What stays on your phone

The app keeps a copy of the records you open on the phone, so it keeps working without signal. It also remembers settings such as the farm you last had open. Signing out does not remove that copy. It stays until the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared in Android's settings.

4. Location

  • The app uses your phone's precise location (GPS) only while the app is open on your screen. It uses it for two things: to show where you are on the map, and to draw a field's boundary while you walk it.
  • It never uses your location in the background. It doesn't ask for background location.
  • Where you have been is not recorded or sent to us. The only positions saved are ones you choose to keep as part of the farm's records, such as a field boundary or the position of a water point.
  • You can refuse location, or turn it off later in Android's settings. The rest of the app still works.

The satellite map comes from Esri. To show it, your phone downloads map images from Esri's servers. Esri therefore receives your IP address and the part of the map you are looking at, as any website you visit would.

As the developer, we can reach the database through Google's console. We look at a holding's records only when its owner or a member asks us for help, to fix a fault, or when the law requires it.

  • If Country Manager is sold or passed to someone else, your information would go with it, still under this policy. We would tell you first.

8. Where it is stored

The database is Google Cloud Firestore, in Google's European multi-region, which uses data centres in the European Union. Information entered on a phone in South Africa is therefore sent to the European Union.

  • Out of South Africa. Section 72 of POPIA allows this when whoever receives the information is bound, by law or by a binding agreement, to protect it substantially as well as POPIA does. The GDPR meets that test.
  • Out of the European Union. Google's sign-in service may process account details in the United States. Google protects such transfers with the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Map requests go from your phone straight to Esri, which may handle them in the United States under its own privacy statement.

9. How long we keep it

  • Your account: until you delete it (see section 10).
  • Farm records: for as long as the holding exists. Its owner decides. Deleting a holding deletes all of its records.
  • A holding nobody is paying for becomes read-only. Its records are not deleted.
  • Records you made on a holding stay with that holding, with your name on them, after you leave it or delete your account. They are the farm's records, not yours alone.
  • Former employees: the app archives employee records rather than deleting them. Section 31 of the Basic Conditions of Employment Act requires an employer to keep them for three years after the last entry. The farm decides when to remove them.
  • Purchase records: seven years, as Dutch tax law requires.
  • Emails you send us: for as long as we need them to deal with what you asked, then deleted.

Anything deleted can stay in Google's backup systems for a limited time before it is overwritten.

10. Deleting your account

In the app: open More → Account Settings → Delete my account. You will be asked for your password, or to sign in with Google again, to prove it is you.

Without the app: follow the steps on our account deletion page.

  • Deleted: your sign-in, your profile (name, email address, phone number, photo link and the record of the terms you accepted), and your membership of every holding.
  • Kept: records you made on a holding, with your name on them, because they belong to the farm. The records are deleted when the holding is deleted.
  • If you own a holding, you must hand it over or delete it first. A holding without an owner cannot be managed by anybody, not even the people still working on it.
  • A subscription is not cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel it in Google Play first.

11. How we protect it

  • Everything sent between the app and Google's servers is encrypted, and Google encrypts the information it stores.
  • Google's servers check every read and every change against the person's membership and role on the holding. A tampered-with copy of the app still cannot open another holding's records.
  • Invite codes can be made to expire and can be withdrawn.
  • Deleting an account requires a fresh password or Google sign-in.
  • Your phone holds a copy of the records you open (see section 3.8). Use a screen lock. Anyone holding your unlocked phone can see what you can see.

If we believe someone has got hold of personal information without permission, we will tell the supervisory authorities and the people affected as the law requires: under the GDPR within 72 hours where it applies, and under section 22 of POPIA as soon as reasonably possible. For farm records, we tell the holding's owner, who is responsible for telling their staff.

12. Your rights

You can:

  • ask whether we hold information about you, and for a copy of it
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date
  • ask us to delete information, or to stop using it for a while
  • ask for the information you gave us in a format a computer can read, to take elsewhere
  • object to our using it on the grounds of our legitimate interest
  • withdraw consent, where we rely on it
  • complain to a supervisory authority (see section 16)

You can do much of this yourself. Edit your profile in Account Settings, take a backup of the farm's records if you are a director or manager, or delete your account. For anything else, email us. We will check that the request really comes from you and answer within one month. We don't charge for this.

If a farm keeps records about you, for example as its employee, ask the farm first, because it decides about those records. If that doesn't work, contact us. We will pass your request to the holding's owner and help them answer it.

13. If you don't use the app yourself

A farm that uses Country Manager may keep records about you if you work for it, supply it, or buy from it (see sections 3.3 and 3.4). The farm is responsible for those records. It should tell you what it keeps and why. You have the rights in section 12.

14. Children

Country Manager is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly give accounts to children. If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

15. Changes to this policy

We will post any new version here and change the date and version at the top. When a change matters, the app asks you to read and accept the new version before you carry on using it.
